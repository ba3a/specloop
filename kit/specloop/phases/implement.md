Implement the specification at `{spec}` following the checklist in `{run_dir}/plan.md`.

- Work test-first: add or update unit tests for each acceptance criterion, then make them pass.
- Tick checklist items in `{run_dir}/plan.md` as you complete them.

Finish your turn when you believe the work is complete. The workflow then runs the test suite; if it fails, you will receive the output and must fix it.
